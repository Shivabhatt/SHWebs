Guest Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for guest registration and AI concierge services.

Effective date: 2026-06-24. This page applies to guest registration, guest web-chat, WhatsApp concierge interactions, and related guest-service workflows provided through SuperHospitality.

1. Scope and guest context

SuperHospitality may provide guest-facing registration, messaging, and AI concierge experiences for participating hotels. These experiences are intended to help guests communicate service requests and receive stay-related support.

2. Information we may process

  • Guest name, mobile number, room number, and booking-linked identifiers needed to validate access and associate the request with a stay.
  • Messages, voice notes, or service-related requests sent through supported channels such as web chat or WhatsApp.
  • Guest-service context such as booking window, hotel name, or room-related support metadata.
  • Technical information used for session handling, fraud prevention, rate limiting, troubleshooting, and service reliability.

3. How we use guest information

  • To register an eligible guest for supported digital concierge services.
  • To provide service-related messaging, assistance, and task routing during the guest's stay.
  • To help hotel staff fulfill guest requests and maintain service records.
  • To protect the platform against abuse, spam, fraud, or unauthorized use.

4. No-spam commitment

We follow a no-spam approach for guest registration and concierge flows. Contact details collected through guest registration are intended for stay-related service delivery, support, and operational communication, not unrelated bulk marketing through this registration flow.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Guest information may be made available to the relevant hotel, authorized hotel staff, communication providers used to deliver concierge messages, and service providers that host or secure the platform. We do not sell guest personal information.

6. Retention and security

We retain guest information for as long as reasonably necessary to support the service, maintain security and service records, address disputes, and comply with legal obligations. We use measures designed to protect information, but no storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Requests and contact

If you have a question about your information, or want to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, you may contact us directly or ask the relevant hotel to coordinate the request.
